# Ghana Trade Balance Analysis (1993-2024) | End-to-End Analytics Project
## Overview
Analyzed three decades of Ghana's trade performance using World Development Indicators (WDI) data to assess trade balance trends, volatility, and its relationship with GDP growth.
**Tech Stack**: Microsoft Excel | SQL | Python (Pandas) | Power BI | WDI

## Methodology
1. **Data Acquisition & Cleaning (Excel)**: Extracted trade and macroeconomic indicators from WDI and performed data cleaning, standardization, and handling of missing values.
2. **Data Transformation (SQL)**: Engineered key metrics including Trade Balance (Total Exports - Total Imports) and calculated year-over-year changes to identify periods of largest volatility.
3. **Statistical Analysis (Python)**: Conducted correlation analysis to quantify the relationship between trade balance and GDP growth.
4. **Visualization & Storytelling (Power BI)**: Developed an interactive dashboard featuring time-series analysis of imports vs. exports, trade balance trajectory, and a scatter plot of trade balance vs GDP growth.
   
## Key Findings
* Ghana maintained a persistent trade deficit from 1993 to 2023, achieving its first significant surplus in 2024.
* Identified periods of highest trade balance volatility through YoY change analysis.
* Established a moderate negative correlation (-0.44) between trade balance and GDP growth.
